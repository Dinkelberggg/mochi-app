Privacy Policy

Mochi for iPhone · Last updated: October 26, 2026 · Deutsch

In short

Mochi needs no account, shows no ads and uses no analytics or tracking services. There are no servers of my own: your data lives on your iPhone and – if you use iCloud – in your iCloud at Apple. As the developer, I can't see it there. Only two things end up somewhere I can access: invite codes for friends (briefly) and reports you send.

2. Data on your device

Everything you create in Mochi – Mochi's look and level, cookies, rooms, habits, reminders, to-dos, lists, mood journal, album, pocket and settings – is stored on your iPhone (in the app's storage and in an app group, so widgets and the share extension can use it). This data only leaves your device as described below.

3. Sync via your iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Mochi syncs your data through the private iCloud database of your Apple Account (Apple CloudKit), so the same Mochi lives on all your devices. The provider is Apple Distribution International Ltd., Ireland. The data is stored in your iCloud storage; I have no access to it. Apple's Privacy Policy applies. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the app features you use).

Mochi also works without iCloud – there's just no sync and no friend features.

4. Friends

Friendships

Each friendship is a separate area in the private iCloud of the person who invited, shared with exactly one other person (Apple CloudKit sharing). It contains Mochi names and looks, levels, postcards with photos and text, waves, recipes, shared shopping lists and game states. Only the two of you can see this data – I can't.

Invite codes

So a friend can find you, Mochi stores a 6-character code in the app's public iCloud database – together with the share link, your Mochi's name and an expiry date. Whoever uses the code briefly stores their anonymous iCloud identifier there (a random string from Apple, not your email address) so that a code only works once. Codes are valid for at most 2 days and are deleted as soon as the friendship is set up or you withdraw the invite. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Reports

You can report postcards, recipes, games, lists and profiles. A report contains the reported content, the reason, your optional comment and the anonymous iCloud identifiers and Mochi names of the people involved. It is stored in the app's public iCloud database, where only I can read it. I use reports solely to review and stop abuse and delete them once they've been handled, at the latest after 12 months. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a safe community).

5. Location, calendar, steps, notifications

These features are optional, off by default and ask for permission first. You can turn them off at any time in Mochi or in the iPhone Settings.

FeatureWhat happens
Weather outfitYour approximate location (to about 1 km) is sent to Apple WeatherKit to get the current weather. Only "rain/sun/…" and the temperature are stored, not the location.
Store detectionMochi uses Apple Maps to find supermarkets near you and lets iOS monitor these places ("Always" permission), so your shopping list appears when you enter a store. Detection runs on the iPhone; I receive no location data.
EventsMochi reads your upcoming calendar events on the device to show them on the home screen and in the widget. They are not transmitted.
WalksMochi reads the iPhone's step count (Motion & Fitness) on the device.
Notifications & alarmsReminders are scheduled locally on the iPhone. Silent notifications from iCloud only tell Mochi that something new arrived from friends.

6. Share extension ("Toss into Pocket")

What you put into Mochi's pocket via the share sheet (photos, files, links, text) is stored only on your device and – if iCloud is on – synced in your private iCloud.

7. Purchases (Mochi Plus)

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. I receive no payment data, only the information from Apple whether a purchase is valid.

8. Crash reports

If you agreed in the iPhone Settings to "Share With App Developers", Apple provides me with anonymous crash and performance reports. You control this under Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

9. Deletion

Under Settings › Delete Data › Delete All My Data you remove your Mochi from this iPhone and from your iCloud, end all friendships and withdraw invite codes. If you use Mochi on several devices, delete it on each one. Deleting the app removes the data on the iPhone; you can additionally remove your iCloud data under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage. For reports you've sent, just email me.

10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Just write to support@fegers.org.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany, www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de.

11. Children

Mochi is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them. Friend features only connect people who give each other a code.

12. Changes

If anything about Mochi changes that affects privacy, I'll update this policy. The version published here applies.
